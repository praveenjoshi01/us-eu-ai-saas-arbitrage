## EU competitor / gap notes
- Larridin: no direct EU competitor found (unverified). Gap: AI Act Art. 4 literacy evidence trail plus GDPR/works-council limits on employee monitoring. Product built for US norms.
- Vera Health: OpenEvidence withdrew from UK/EU ~Apr 2026 (reason reported as regulatory uncertainty, unconfirmed). EU players: DR.INFO (CE-marked), AMBOSS, Medwise, ClariMed, Praxis Medicine (Balderton/Creandum, not shipping). Crowded; Vera itself markets EU availability. Gap: MDR/CE, health-data residency, language/guidelines.
- Collective: Finom (EU AI accounting agent, Germany first) and many local players. Gap is per-country tax/VAT rules and language, which favors a country-by-country entrant but is costly.
